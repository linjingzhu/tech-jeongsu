## How the documents are made

- **Written with AI tools.** AI tools help with drafting, research and translation. This is stated openly.
- **Sources are kept.** Facts that can change carry a date and a source, with links in each document's references.
- **Reviewed adversarially.** A different model from the one that wrote a document reviews it by trying to disprove its scope, depth, examples and accuracy. Findings are fixed and the document is reviewed again.
- **Errors get fixed.** If you find a mistake, tell me using the method on the contact page. I check it and correct the document.

## Things to keep in mind

- The documents are general learning material. The Business section in particular is not legal or tax advice, so confirm with the official authority or a professional before acting.
- Tool features, prices and policies change often. Check each document's reference date and official sources.
- External links open in a new tab. Linked sites are responsible for their own content.
